## 3D and image sequences

- `public/models/lourdes.glb`: own work. Exported from our Blender model of the GA (`blender/lourdes/scripts/lourdes_export_glb.py` → `blender/lourdes/exports/Lourdes_web.glb`, Draco-compressed, no drone docks), then passed through `scripts/strip-glb-extras.py`, which removes builder metadata (glTF `extras`) and leaves geometry and names unchanged. The viewer hides the translucent tank volumes (`SYS_TANKS`) used by the cutaway. Since 13 October (owner request) the 24 containers carry the white BENNET AND BERNARD SUBSEA livery: one embedded PNG texture (`bnb_container_logo_web`, 2048×256, 29,545 bytes; our redraw of the owner's livery reference `references_supplied/BNB_container_livery_reference.jpg`, made by `blender/lourdes/decals/make_container_logo_web.py`). 771,148 bytes (761,696 before the livery).
- `public/sequences/lourdes/`: the scroll story. Own work: 360 frames rendered from `blender/lourdes/Lourdes_Story.blend` (no drone docks), encoded by `tools/encode_sequence.py` into `lg/` (360 × 1600×900 WebP, 16,185,908 bytes), `sm/` (181 × 960×540 WebP, 3,423,058 bytes), two posters, ten chapter stills and `manifest.json`. Frames 223–349 were re-rendered on 12 October (review: the wheelhouse push-in stops further out and the deckhouse closes before the close-up); lg 16,057,074 bytes and sm 3,391,598 bytes since then. All 360 frames were re-rendered on 13 October with the white container livery (owner request; camera, timing, light and chapters unchanged): lg 15,948,162 bytes, sm 3,461,900 bytes.
